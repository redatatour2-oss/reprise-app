Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-01

Summary

Reprise does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your cards, reviews and settings are stored on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your stacks, cards, review history, settings and exam dates.

Pages you scan with the camera are read on your iPhone and are not uploaded.

When you answer out loud, your voice is turned into text on your iPhone. Reprise asks for on-device recognition only. If a language is not available on-device, voice answers are not offered in it, and nothing is sent away instead.

Permissions

Camera: to scan pages into cards.

Microphone and Speech Recognition: for Voice Examiner.

You can turn any of these off in iOS Settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Reprise never sees your payment details or Apple Account. The app checks your purchase status with Apple's StoreKit on your device to unlock Premium.

What we receive

Nothing. We run no servers for the app and receive no personal data from it. If you email us for support, we use your message and address only to reply.

Backups

Your data is part of your iPhone backup if you use iCloud or computer backups. Files you export are saved where you choose; sharing them is up to you.

Children

Reprise collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be posted on this page. Questions: redatatour2@gmail.com.
